# Team Challenges

### Challenge 1 - Emotional Intelligence

#### Recognizing team members

* You are going to train a deep learning algorithm to recognize each of you.
* Training a model means teaching it to identify patterns from examples.

--

### Challenge 1 - Emotional Intelligence

* You will train **one class per team member**
  * ⚠️ plus an empty one
* Take different pictures of each of you to let the algorithm learn.
* Pay attention when you wear sunglasses, hats...
  * The camera needs to recognize your face in any case!

--

### Challenge 2 - Rock / Paper / Scissor

* Train the computer to be able to play ✊✌️👋
* Take pictures of different hands and angles to let the system recognize the symbol.
  * ⚠️ plus an empty one
* Be careful with changes to the background.
