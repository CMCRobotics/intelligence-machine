## Emotional Intelligence

### Challenge 1

#### Recognise the members of the team
--

You are going to train a deep learning algorithm to recognise each of you
--

Training a model means teaching it to identify patterns from examples.
--

You will have different classes — one class with each of your names.
--

Take different pictures of each of you to let the algorithm learn.
--

Pay attention also when you wear sunglasses, hats, etc. — the camera needs to recognise your face in any case!

## Verbal Intelligence

### Challenge 3

#### Create the Word Challenge
--

Now we want you to be creative! The machine will have to decide which team wins.
--

You have to create a new word that doesn’t exist yet.
--

The machine is going to evaluate your creativity, multilinguality, and adherence to the topic!
--

Create a word that expresses: **the emotion the first astronaut felt when he went on the Moon.**

## Auditory Intelligence

### Challenge 4

#### Auditory Recognition
--

Let's train a model that can recognise a word even if it is not perfectly pronounced!
--

The words are:

2. Italian: *elettromagnetismo*
